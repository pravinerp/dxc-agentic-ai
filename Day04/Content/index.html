Concept 2 · INTERMEDIATE → ADVANCED

GraphRAG (awareness) and agentic RAG

Lab 3 improved one search. Some questions need more than one: facts spread across articles, or an answer that depends on a first answer. Two ideas address that.

Awareness only, 5 minutes. GraphRAG stores entities and relationships (a knowledge graph) next to the text. It shines on "who approves what, and for how long" multi-hop questions. It costs more to build, so use it where relationships matter.
Question: A contractor needs VPN. Who must approve it, and how long does it last? Click Next hop.
Press Next hop to start at the entity "Contractor".

Plain chunk RAG may retrieve KB-005 or KB-015, but not both. The graph links them, so one traversal gathers the whole chain.

Idea: after retrieving, a critic asks "is this enough to answer every part?". If not, the agent rewrites the query and searches again. Simple questions skip the loop (adaptive routing). Loops are capped, and a final answer check sends ungrounded answers to a human.
Question: "I left my laptop at the airport. What will the helpdesk do with it, and how fast will they respond?"
🧭 Router
Two parts ("what" and "how fast") → complex: use the critic loop.
🔎 Round 1
Retrieve and rerank → KB-014 Lost or Stolen Device, plus two weaker hits.
🧐 Critic
Not enough: the passages say what the helpdesk does, but not how fast it responds.
✏️ Rewrite
New query: "first response time for a Critical priority ticket".
🔎 Round 2
Retrieve → KB-016 Ticket Priority and SLAs joins the evidence.
🧐 Critic
Enough evidence. Loop ends (the cap is 2 rounds).
💬 Answer
"They lock and remotely wipe the device [KB-014]. Lost devices are always Critical: first response 15 minutes [KB-016]."
🛡️ Check
Second critic: every claim is in the sources → grounded. (If not: hand over to a human.)
Idea: every extra round is more LLM calls, more latency, more cost. The router keeps simple questions cheap. Illustrative numbers: your Lab 4 Compare tab shows the real ones.
LLM calls / question, single pass2.0
LLM calls / question, agentic0
Cost multiple×1.0
Slowest question (s)0

Single pass = rerank + answer (2 calls). A complex question with R rounds = R × (rerank + critic) + (R − 1) rewrites + answer + answer check. Assumes ~1.2 s per call.
