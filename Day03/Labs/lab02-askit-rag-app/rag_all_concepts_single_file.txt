Cafeteria operations cover meal planning, kitchen hygiene, vendor schedules, food counters, seating arrangements, and waste collection. Employees should use approved meal coupons where applicable. Event catering requests need advance notice and manager confirmation.

Office parking is managed by workplace services. Employees should use designated parking levels, display valid authorization, and avoid visitor spaces. Bicycle parking is available near selected entrances.

Travel claims require receipts, cost-center information, manager approval, and submission through the expense portal. Hotel invoices, taxi receipts, and approved meal expenses should be attached before submission.

Meeting rooms include displays, microphones, speakers, booking panels, whiteboards, and control systems. Facilities teams manage room layouts and physical equipment. Large meetings may require advance booking.

Printer services cover toner replacement, paper loading, print queues, duplex settings, color restrictions, and device maintenance. High-volume print jobs should be scheduled outside peak periods.

Learning programs include virtual sessions, classroom training, self-paced modules, assessments, certifications, attendance reporting, and feedback surveys. Enrollment depends on role and manager approval.

Building badges are issued by workplace services. Lost badges should be reported to security. Visitor badges must be returned after use.

Procurement requests require an approved supplier, cost center, business justification, and financial authorization. Purchase orders must be created before certain categories of spend.

Payroll issue summary. If an employee reports a wrong salary and requires an urgent payroll correction, the case should be escalated to Payroll Operations through the HR service desk. This summary describes the urgent payroll correction process but does not provide a correction code.
